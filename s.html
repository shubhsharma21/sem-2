The Indian Premier League (IPL) has revolutionized professional cricket since its inception in 2008 by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI), transforming the sport into a high-octane blend of entertainment and top-tier competition. As the most popular and lucrative T20 league globally, it features city-based franchise teams—such as the five-time winners Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings—that attract elite international talent alongside emerging Indian stars. Held annually between March and May, the league has become a massive commercial success, driving significant revenue through broadcasting deals and sponsorships while providing a vital platform for young players to launch their international careers. Despite occasional controversies, the IPL’s unique, fast-paced format and, as of 2025, a ten-team structure, continues to captivate millions of fans, making it a cornerstone of modern cricket and a major contributor to the Indian economy.
